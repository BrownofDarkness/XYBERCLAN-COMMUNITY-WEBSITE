## 0. Before we start (read this to the person you interview)

We are rebuilding the Xyberclan website. We want it to feel like Xyberclan and not like any other
community. So we build it only from things that are **true** about the clan. The founders have answered
the same kind of questions; we also want to hear how **you** live the clan from the inside. Your answers
go into one file, `PRODUCT.md`, and every other part of the project will use that file.

**Rules**
- **Never guess.** If you do not know, answer "unknown" and tell us who might know.
- **Answer in full sentences.**
- **Every number and every link needs a date and a source.** Example: `214 members (Discord, 2026-11-20)`.
- **We only name people who have given their consent.** Ask them first if you are not sure.
- **Time:** about 30 minutes, by voice or chat.
- **Privacy:** your raw answers stay out of the code repository. Only a summary goes in.
