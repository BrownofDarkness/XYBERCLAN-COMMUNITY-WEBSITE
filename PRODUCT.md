## In one sentence

**What it is (Q3):** Xyberclan is a community of young African developers, hackers, network people and
designers who learn from each other, share quality resources, connect and collaborate. It started in
Cameroon and is for Africa and for the world. (Founder interview, 2026-11-20)

**What it is not (Q3):** Xyberclan is not a profit organisation, not an incubator, and not a place that
keeps knowledge for itself; it exists to help everyone in tech. *(wording to confirm)*
(Founder interview, 2026-11-20)

## The name

- **(Q1)** "Xyberclan" is pronounced like "Cyberclan". (Founder interview, 2026-11-20)
- **(Q1)** The three co-founders of the startup chose the name together in November 2024. They first
  wanted "Cyberclan", but the name was already taken. (Founder interview, 2026-11-20)
- **(Q1)** They kept the idea of a digital group, a clan, and replaced the C with an X. The X stands for
  their starting point and for what they left behind, which they crossed out. (Founder interview, 2026-11-20)
- **(Q4)** The startup was founded by three co-founders. The community was built by all the members of
  the startup. No individual names are given. (Founder interview, 2026-11-20)

## Where we live

**Places (Q7, Q11)**

- **Home city:** Yaoundé, Cameroon. (Founder interview, 2026-11-20)

## Rhythm and rituals

- **(Q12)** The clan has no fixed weekly or monthly ritual. Workshops, tech events and meetups happen
  when they are scheduled, online or in person. Group discussions happen online. (Founder interview, 2026-11-21)
- **(Q13)** No inside joke, word or habit was named. The shared humour is tech memes and jokes; new
  members join all the time. (Founder interview, 2026-11-21)
