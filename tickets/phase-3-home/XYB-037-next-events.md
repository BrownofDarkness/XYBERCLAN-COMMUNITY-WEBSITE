# XYB-037 — Next events: the tickets

## Why this exists (the strategy)
**Claim:** you don't have to wait to belong. There is a date coming up where you can show up and meet us.

**Proof:** the next real events, each designed as a **ticket stub**: name, date, time, place, and a button
to save your spot. A ticket is the printed object of the event world; it turns "an event list" into "an
invitation in your hand".

## What the visitor should feel and do
- **Feel:** invited. "I could actually go to that."
- **Do:** press **Save my spot** on one event, or add it to their calendar.

## Scope
- Heading + line, then the next 1–3 upcoming events as ticket stubs, then a link to all events.
- Each ticket: event name, type (meetup / workshop / build night / online), date, time with time zone,
  place (venue + city, or "Online"), 1–2 host blobs, **Save my spot** (RSVP link) and **Add to calendar** (.ics).
- An empty state when nothing is scheduled.

## Content
- From `content/events/` (XYB-005): only events with `startsAt` in the future, sorted by date, max 3.
- Each event file provides: `title`, `kind`, `startsAt`, `endsAt`, `timezone`, `venue`, `city`, `online`,
  `rsvpUrl`, `hosts` (member handles), `summary` (one sentence), `slug`.
- Heading (draft — confirm in XYB-018): "Come say hi in person".
- Line: "Meetups, workshops and build nights. Bring a friend, or come alone and leave with ten."
- **Empty state** (draft): "Nothing on the calendar yet. Want to be told first?" + link to the Discord
  announcements channel or newsletter (whichever the clan really has, XYB-001).
- Owner: event organisers add event files; copy from XYB-018.

## Design spec

**Desktop (1440)**
- **Lagoon field.** Heading `h2` at display step 2 on the left (columns 1–6), line on the right (columns 8–12).
- Tickets laid out as a **slightly fanned stack**, as if three tickets were dropped on a table: the first one
  biggest and in front (columns 2–8), the next ones offset behind it to the right, rotated −3° / +2°. The
  front ticket is fully readable; the back ones show their dates and titles.
- **Ticket anatomy** (paper-coloured shape on the Lagoon field):
  - A real ticket shape: rectangle with a **perforated tear line** (a row of punched semicircle notches cut
    from the edges, made with CSS mask — *not* a dashed line), splitting the ticket into a main part (2/3)
    and a stub (1/3).
  - Main part: kind (as a word in the sentence, e.g. "Workshop in Douala"), title at display step 4,
    summary at text size, hosts' blobs + names.
  - Stub: the **date set big** (day number at display step 2, month and weekday at text-large), time + time
    zone, venue/city. The stub is where the eye goes first.
  - Actions under the ticket: **Save my spot** (ink pill) and **Add to calendar** (text link).
- Link under the stack: **See all events →** `/events`.

**Tablet (768):** tickets side by side in a horizontal swipe row (no rotation), stub on the right of each.
**Phone (390):** tickets stack vertically, stub on **top** (date first), rotation removed, full width.

**One event only:** a single large ticket, centred on the grid columns 2–11.

## Motion spec
- **Entrance:** tickets **slide in from the right and drop onto the "table"** one by one (translate + rotate,
  then a small settle), 120ms stagger; the hosts' blobs pop up on the ticket after it lands.
- **Hover a back ticket (desktop):** it lifts (translateY −12px, rotate toward 0°) to show more; click brings it
  to the front (the stack reorders with a FLIP animation, 450ms).
- **Save my spot press:** the stub "tears" a little (the stub rotates 4° around the perforation and springs back)
  before the link opens. Purely visual; the link opens immediately.
- **Idle:** hosts' blobs bob on the clan beat.
- **Reduced motion:** no fan, no rotation, no tear; tickets sit in a simple row/stack.

## Build notes
- Files: `components/home/NextEvents.tsx`, `components/events/Ticket.tsx` (shared with XYB-054/055).
- Perforation: CSS `mask` with radial-gradient circles at the tear line is allowed (it's a cut-out shape, not a
  visible gradient). Provide a fallback straight edge if `mask` is unsupported.
- Dates: always format with the event's `timezone` using `Intl.DateTimeFormat`; show the zone abbreviation.
  Never use the visitor's local zone for the printed date (people need the real local time of the venue).
- `.ics`: generated by a route `app/events/[slug]/ics/route.ts` (or a static file at build time).
- RSVP link: external → ↗, opens in the same tab.
- Each ticket is an `<article aria-labelledby>`; the date is a `<time datetime>`.
- Filtering "future only" happens at request time (ISR every hour) so past events disappear without a rebuild.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Only future events appear, max 3, nearest first; past events disappear within an hour without a deploy.
- [ ] Dates and times show in the event's own time zone, with the zone shown.
- [ ] The ticket has a real perforation cut-out (mask), not a dashed or dotted line.
- [ ] Save my spot opens the real RSVP link; Add to calendar downloads a valid `.ics` (opens in Google
      Calendar and Apple Calendar).
- [ ] The empty state shows when no events are upcoming, with a real link.
- [ ] Desktop fan: every ticket's date and title is readable; clicking a back ticket brings it forward.
- [ ] Keyboard: every ticket's actions are reachable in order; bringing a ticket forward works with Enter.
- [ ] Phone: date stub is on top; nothing rotated.
- [ ] Text on paper tickets and on the Lagoon field meets contrast rules.
- [ ] Reduced motion: no fan, rotation or tear.
- [ ] Bilingual: every visible string comes from `messages/*.json` or localized content (XYB-006); the section works in English and French, and every headline still fits two lines in French at 1440, 768 and 390.
- [ ] Follows the house rules in `docs/creative-direction.md` §4.

## Out of scope
- Event index and detail pages (XYB-054, XYB-055). An RSVP system of our own (we link to the real one).
